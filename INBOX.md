Книга:
This is **И. И. Осипова, «Хасиды: “Спасая народ свой…”»** (Irina Ivanovna Osipova, *Hasidim: “Saving Their People…”*), Moscow: **Формика-С**, **2002**, **293 pp.**, ISBN **5-89737-134-2** (also listed as 978-5-89737-134-1). Softcover. Artist: Mark Ibshman. It is a documentary history of the Chabad/Hasidic underground under Soviet repression, based on OGPU–NKVD–MGB files plus a memorial list of 232 people.

It was a small 2002 print run. It is **out of print**. There is no current new-book listing on Ozon, Labirint, or the usual Judaica shops.

## Physical copy

Treat it as a used-book hunt, not a cart checkout.

**Russian secondhand (best chance of a paper copy)**  
Search the exact title plus ISBN:

- [Meshok](https://meshok.net/)
- [Avito](https://www.avito.ru/)
- [Alib.ru](https://www.alib.ru/)
- [Books.ru](https://www.books.ru/) / smaller antiquarian shops

Use: `Осипова Хасиды Спасая народ свой 5-89737-134-2`

Expect a used paperback if anything appears; prices vary and shipping from Russia to Massachusetts is slow and sometimes restricted.

**US / international used**  
[Globus Books](https://www.globusbooks.com/) in San Francisco had a very good copy and marked it **sold**. They specialize in Russian Judaica; worth emailing them to ask if another copy comes in.

Also check:

- AbeBooks / Biblio / eBay: `Osipova Hasidy Formika 2002` or the ISBN  
- Boston-area used Judaica: Israel Book Shop (Brookline), local shul libraries, Brandeis/Harvard Judaica stacks via WorldCat rather than purchase

**Libraries first if you only need to read it**  
Search WorldCat for the ISBN. Research libraries with strong Russian or Jewish collections sometimes hold it. Request via interlibrary loan from a Newton/Boston public or university card.
